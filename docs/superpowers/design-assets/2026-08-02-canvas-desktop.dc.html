2All three, reconciled

They aren't three apps — they're one app with a switch and a rail. 1b and 1c are the same data at two densities, so they become a Cards / Ledger toggle in the toolbar (persist it in Router next to sidebar, and it doubles as the "not customizable" fix). 1d's Sandbox stops being a per-course inspector and becomes a rail that follows you: on the Dashboard it solves for your term GPA, inside a course it solves for that course — same CalculatorViewModel, one scope parameter. And 1d's dark treatment becomes the app's dark mode rather than a separate direction. Everything below is light/native chrome carrying 1c's ink-and-hatch grade language.
